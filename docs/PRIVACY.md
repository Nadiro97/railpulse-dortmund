# Privacy and secure publication

- This project studies public train-stop events and station-level timetables, not people.
- No passenger, mobile-device, card, employee, driver or account data is collected.
- No secrets or credentials should be stored in source control.
- Raw national datasets, extracted stop-level records, generated local CSV reports and databases are ignored by Git.
- Publish aggregated statistics and visualizations with appropriate source attribution. Inspect any output before distributing.
- New datasets require independent licence and privacy review, especially if identifiers can be joined to identifiable individuals.
- Deletion and retention of local downloaded data are under the user's control.

This policy minimizes privacy risk but is not a legal determination of GDPR applicability to all future extensions.
